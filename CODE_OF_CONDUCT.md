# Community code of conduct

Participants in Crossing issues, pull requests and project discussions should
make technical collaboration respectful and accessible. This policy applies to
maintainers, contributors and users in project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

Discuss the work and evidence, explain disagreement clearly and respect other
participants' experience and time. Accept corrections, give credit for others'
work and avoid assumptions about a person's identity or circumstances. Keep
financial claims and security discussions precise.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, deliberate intimidation, publication
of another person's private information, impersonation and repeated targeted
disruption are unacceptable. Do not use issue discussions to pressure someone
to reveal wallet secrets, verification records or exploitable security details.

Good-faith technical disagreement and reproducible bug reports are welcome.
Disagreement alone is not misconduct.

## Reporting and moderation

For a conduct concern, contact a maintainer listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) privately rather than posting personal
information publicly. The PoC has no dedicated conduct email or separate
conduct-response team. Do not use public issues for sensitive allegations.
Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Repository owners may remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict a
discussion or suspend access to project spaces. Choose a response proportionate
to the behavior, preserve relevant evidence privately and communicate the reason
to affected participants when appropriate. Immediate restrictions may be needed
for threats, exposed personal information or active abuse.

A person with a conflict of interest should not be the sole reviewer of the
complaint. Where another repository owner is available, request their review of
a moderation appeal. The PoC does not promise an independent appeals body or
response deadline. A production community should publish a reachable private
contact and identify who can handle conflicts before expanding participation.

Handle reports on a need-to-know basis. Do not promise secrecy beyond what the
reporting channel and necessary investigation can provide. This policy governs
participation in project spaces; it does not change the software license or
permissions in deployed contracts.
